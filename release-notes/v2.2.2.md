## ACIES Scheduler 2.2.2

### What is new
**Publish**
- **Partial Print.** When Publish asks whether to refresh Excel links or quick print, a new Partial Print choice lets you print only the sheets you pick. It lists the DWGs in the project's discipline folder, and you can browse for other drawings. You can also refresh Excel links in just the selected drawings first.

**CAD tools**
- **Repair XREF Paths now saves new paths.** It used to report "XREF paths could not be fully verified" and leave the drawing unchanged. It now repoints each XREF the same way AutoCAD's XREF Manager does, reloads it, and confirms the new path was saved.
- **Repair XREF Paths scans the whole project.** With a project selected, it scans every DWG in the project's discipline folder instead of asking for files. Choose... opens in the project's Xrefs folder.
- **Tool dialogs no longer open hidden.** Repair XREF Paths, Publish's paper size check, Freeze / Thaw and the XREF ZIP picker could open their window invisibly and leave the tool waiting. They now always appear.
- **Clean Drawings flattens PDF markups.** An attached PDF with markups or form fields no longer stops the run. The markups are flattened into the working copy, and the original PDF is not changed.

Installing over 2.2.1 keeps your projects and settings.
